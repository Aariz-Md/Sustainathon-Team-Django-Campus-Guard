KEY TECHNICAL DECISIONS
-----------------------
1. MSE-based similarity instead of SSIM (no C compiler available)
2. CLAHE preprocessing for lighting normalization
3. 2.0s persistence threshold to filter brief occlusions
4. Global occlusion guard: ≥3 slots changing within 1s triggers 3s freeze
5. FFmpeg with H.264/yuv420p/faststart for browser-compatible clips
